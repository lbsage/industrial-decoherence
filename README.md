# Industrial Decoherence: The Unified Diagnosis

**By LaSalle Browne · AlphaSage**

An interactive long-form website mapping America's capability formation 
crisis across five domains — manufacturing, infrastructure, AI, education, 
and national resilience — and the strategic architecture to address it.

## What's Inside

- **Interactive Symptom Map** — Five domains, six root causes, one underlying 
  pathology. Click any node to explore connections and symptom profiles.
- **Evidence Trail** — Thirteen sources synthesized into a unified thesis, 
  from Leon Liao's closed-loop analysis of China's industrial system to 
  RAND's pharmaceutical supply chain research.
- **Diagnostic Chokepoints** — Transformers, GaN, rare earths, 
  pharmaceutical APIs, ocean sovereignty, and the sulphur cascade visualized.
- **Seven-Layer Strategic Blueprint** — From cultural rewiring through 
  substrate sovereignty, with concrete pilots and coalition architecture.
- **Human Development Pipeline** — The Education Convergence Architecture 
  applied to manufacturing: how capability is actually formed, not just 
  trained.

## The Core Thesis

America does not have an innovation problem. It does not have a capital 
problem. It does not even, at its core, have a manufacturing problem.

It has a capability formation problem — the progressive loss of the 
individual, organizational, and institutional capacity to build hard things 
at scale, reliably, over time. Until that is understood, every solution will 
feel directionally correct and still fall short.
